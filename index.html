Lung simulator for a low-cost ventilator

My final-year project at NYP (2022, supervised by Dr. Tan Seng Sing). Mechanical ventilators are too expensive for many hospitals in low- and middle-income countries, so the team built one from off-the-shelf parts — and my job was the lung simulator used to test it. I built the electronics (PCB, potentiometers, LCD, pressure sensor, Arduino control states), 3D printed the enclosure and re-cut parts with vernier caliper measurements until everything fitted, and prototyped a lung-deflation assist from acrylic and elastic bands — testing 2, 4 and 6 bands to mimic how a real lung keeps reserve air. I tracked air leaks with the soap-and-water test and helped code the ventilator's backup breathing mode for when no breath is detected.

Building Structo 3D dental printers

During my internship at Kinergy I helped build Structo DentaForm printers — orthodontic 3D printers that print dental arches used for invisible aligners. I did sub-assemblies and electrical integration: wiring harnesses, soldering, crimping, and cable management inside the machine. The highlight: I designed and built a jig that brought consistency to one of the sub-assemblies, measuring carefully and refining the design with feedback from colleagues until the build was repeatable.
